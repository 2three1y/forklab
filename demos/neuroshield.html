Neuroshield

The real app is a Chrome extension that protects people with photosensitive epilepsy by spotting flashing and strobing on websites and blocking it.

This is a fan-made pretend version, not the real app. Everything runs on this page with fake data. Nothing is sent anywhere.

This page never flashes or blinks. The pretend videos are only numbers: how many flashes happen each second.

Try it

Pick a pretend video and a protection level, then play it. Each second, the shield counts the flashes. Too many flashes in one second and the shield dims and pauses the video. What happens is written in the log below.
