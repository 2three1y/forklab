Social Catalysts

The real project is the data and code from a Facebook research paper about social catalysts: people whose posts get their friends talking to each other, even friends who did not know each other.

This is a fan-made pretend version, not the real app. Everything runs on this page with fake data. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Try it

Five made-up friends post things, and you can join in. A post counts as a catalyst post when two of the poster's friends end up talking to each other in the comments. Find the social catalysts, then make a post as yourself and see your score change.
