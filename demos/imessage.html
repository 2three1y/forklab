mautrix-imessage

The real app is a bridge that connects iMessage to Matrix, so you can text iPhone people from a Matrix chat app like Element.

This is a fan-made pretend version, not the real app. Everything runs on this page with fake data. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The real bridge needs a Mac (or a jailbroken iPhone) that stays on all the time, plus a Matrix server.

Try it

Two sides of one chat with Mom. Pick which side you are sending from. Each message shows up on that side first, then the pretend bridge copies it to the other side. Mom answers from her iPhone and the bridge carries her reply to Matrix too.

Matrix side: Element app (newest at the bottom)

    iMessage side: Messages app (newest at the bottom)
